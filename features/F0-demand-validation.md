# F0 - Demand validation

Status: planned. Dependencies: access to people and permissioned examples. Completion evidence: none.

## Outcome and scope

Determine whether the report solves a repeated problem beyond native metrics and whether a buyer pays for help. Follow [VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md). Engineering can proceed when directly requested; missing interviews do not require pretending this feature is complete.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] F0-A1: Record 10 actual conversations, including role, recent workflow, current alternatives and pain; anonymize public notes.
- [ ] F0-A2: Three teams review permissioned reports and record specific useful and irrelevant findings.
- [ ] F0-A3: Record whether two teams acted on a finding, with what changed and supporting evidence; count only actual actions.
- [ ] F0-A4: Record at least one settled paid audit/implementation or explicitly conclude this commercial threshold was not met. A negative result completes the research criterion; it does not validate willingness to pay.
- [ ] F0-A5: Write a continue/refine/services-only/stop recommendation supported by the observations and limitations.

## Proof and failure cases

Use dated notes, permissioned feedback, actual changes and redacted payment confirmation. No invented identities/quotes. A competitor pricing page, waitlist signup, unpaid invoice, and enthusiastic message are not proof of payment. If recruitment is unavailable, preserve the unanswered criteria and next action.

## Handoff

Link research evidence, recommendation and outstanding experiments. Track research completion separately from positive validation.
